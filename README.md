# Alphabet Equity Research Valuation
An independent analysis of whether Alphabet's growth in Google Cloud and AI generate sufficient cash flow to justify Alphabet's capital investment while protecting profitability in its search business. The analysis evaluates Alphabet's business, financial performance and estimated intrinsic value using a discounted cash flow valuation and comparable company analysis.
## 1. Project Overview
**Research question:** Can growth in Google Cloud & AI monetisation support Alphabet’s valuation while sustaining Search profitability? 

**Motivation:** I undertook this project with the aim of developing my equity research and financial modelling skills by applying corporate finance concepts to a real company. Through financial statement analysis, forecasting and valuation, I aim to strengthen my ability to translate business insights into justified financial assumptions and an evidence-based investment conclusion.

**Why Alphabet?** Being Google's parent company, Alphabet, is one of the world's largest and most influential companies. Alphabet combines an established advertising business with growth opportunities in cloud computing and AI. This presents an interesting valuation question: whether growth in Google Cloud and AI justifies the investment required. Analysing Alphabet allows me to explore how growth, competitive pressures, capital expenditure and profitability interact to influence shareholder value.

**Objective:** The project aims to estimate Alphabet’s intrinsic value using discounted cash flow (DCF) valuation, supported by comparable company analysis, Bull, base and bear scenarios, alongside sensitivity analysis, will assess how changes in key assumptions affect estimated value per share and identify the principal drivers of the investment conclusion.
